A single 1024x1024 front page that sums up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026 in six drawn panels: Ukraine's sea drones sink a Russian navy corvette at a base off Libya; the COP31 climate talks in Antalya end without a deal after record-long negotiations; shipping attacks in the Strait of Hormuz reach their highest level of the war, with seven tankers hit in a week; Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO and the S&P 500 leaps about 5%; a fire at a candy factory in Indonesia kills at least 24 people with 34 workers missing; and the European-Japanese spacecraft BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury after an eight-year journey. The picture is drawn from scratch by `draw.py` (Pillow primitives only, no photographs) and committed as `entry.png`; `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
